## Implemented response to these actual failures

1. A visual step may include an answer. Its claim receives the visual audit and
   its answer receives an independent entailment/option-mapping check.
2. Removed placeholder JSON strings that small models copied verbatim.
3. Localization now belongs to a separate call; internal normalized ledger boxes
   are not fed to the proposer as pixel-coordinate examples.
4. Repair changes evidence: independently reacquire a region and inspect full
   scene plus enlarged crop. Three attempts progressively broaden crop context.
5. CF masks are propagated into both global and zoomed views; no unmasked crop
   leaks the evidence being removed. Total image-token budget is held fixed.
6. Model instances persist between development jobs; stateless controller/prompt
   revisions reload between jobs, avoiding repeated 8–11 minute weight loads.

New job results must be checked under `runtime/workers/{7b_dev,3b_dev}/results`.
Old traces are not relabeled as results of the repaired method.
